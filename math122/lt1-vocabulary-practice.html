Practice identifying populations, samples, variables, parameters, statistics, observations, censuses, descriptive statistics, and statistical inference.

Learning target: I can identify the population, sample, variable, parameter, and statistic in a statistical study and distinguish descriptive statistics from statistical inference.

Review the vocabulary

Population

The entire group the study is interested in.

Sample

The subset of the population from which data are collected.

Variable

The characteristic recorded for each individual.

Parameter

A numerical summary describing the population.

Statistic

A numerical summary calculated from a sample.

Observation

A recorded value for one individual.

Census

Data are collected from every individual in the population.

Descriptive statistics

Summarize the data that were actually observed.

Statistical inference

Use sample results to draw a conclusion about a population.
