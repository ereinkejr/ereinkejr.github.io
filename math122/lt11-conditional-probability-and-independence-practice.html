Learning Target 11 · LT11

Conditional Probability and Independence

I can calculate and interpret simple conditional probabilities, decide whether events are independent, and distinguish independence from mutual exclusivity.

Quick reference

Conditional probabilityP(A | B) = number in both A and B ÷ number in B. Focus on the group after “given.”
IndependenceEvents are independent when knowing one occurred does not change the probability of the other.
Mutual exclusivityEvents are mutually exclusive if they cannot occur together.
Without replacementThe first draw changes the contents of the bag, so the next probability may change.
