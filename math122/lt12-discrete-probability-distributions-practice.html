Discrete Probability Distributions

Define a discrete random variable, check whether a proposed distribution is valid, and calculate probabilities from a distribution.

Quick reference

Discrete random variableAssigns a numerical value to an outcome, with finite or countably infinite possible values.
Valid distribution\(0\leq P(X=x)\leq 1\) and \(\sum_x P(X=x)=1\).
Notation\(P(X=x)\) is the probability that \(X\) equals exactly \(x\).
Several valuesAdd their probabilities. At most includes the endpoint; less than excludes it.
