Expected Value and Variation

Calculate and interpret expected value, variance, and standard deviation of discrete random variables, and use them to compare distributions.

Quick reference

Expected valueμ=E(X)=Σ xP(X=x). The long-run average.
Varianceσ²=Σ x²P(X=x)−μ².
Standard deviationσ=√(σ²). It uses the original measurement units.
Expected net gainExpected prize minus entry fee. A negative value means a long-run average loss.

Use a calculator as needed. Keep full precision in intermediate steps; round final answers to two decimal places when needed.
