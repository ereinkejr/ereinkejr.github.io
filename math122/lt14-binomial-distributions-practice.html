Learning Target 14 · LT14

Binomial Distributions

Determine whether binomial conditions hold and calculate and interpret binomial probabilities, mean, and standard deviation.

Quick review

ConditionsFixed n, independent trials, success/failure, constant p.
Probability\(P(X=k)=\binom{n}{k}p^{k}(1-p)^{n-k}\)
Mean\(\mu=np\)
SD\(\sigma=\sqrt{np(1-p)}\)

“Success” is the outcome counted, not necessarily a desirable outcome. Round probabilities to four decimals and SDs to two decimals.

Reference: heads in four fair coin tosses
