Poisson Distributions

Recognize Poisson situations, adjust expected counts for intervals, and calculate and interpret probabilities.

Quick reference

When to use PoissonCount occurrences in a fixed interval, with constant rate, independent counts in nonoverlapping intervals, and negligible chance of multiple events in a tiny interval.
Interval meanλ = (rate) × (interval size). Match units.
Exact probabilityP(X=k) = e−λλk/k!
Mean and SDμ=λ, σ=√λ

Use a calculator if needed. Round probabilities to four decimals and standard deviations to two decimals.
