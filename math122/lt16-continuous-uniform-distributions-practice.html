Learning Target 16 · LT16

Continuous Uniform Distributions

Recognize a continuous uniform model and calculate and interpret interval probabilities, the mean, and the standard deviation.

Quick reference

Density height\(f(x)=\frac{1}{b-a}\) for \(a\leq x\leq b\); zero outside.
Interval probability\(P(c\leq X\leq d)=\frac{d-c}{b-a}\), for intervals inside \([a,b]\).
Mean\(\mu=\frac{a+b}{2}\)
Standard deviation\(\sigma=\frac{b-a}{\sqrt{12}}\)
Single exact value\(P(X=c)=0\); endpoints do not change probability.
Important distinctionProbability is area under density, not height.
