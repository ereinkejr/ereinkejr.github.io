Learning Target 17 · LT17

Exponential Distributions

Calculate and interpret continuous waiting-time probabilities, distinguish rate from mean, find percentiles, and explain memorylessness.

Quick reference

Rate and meanμ=1/λ; λ=1/μ
Wait longer than tP(T>t)=e−λt
Wait at most tP(T≤t)=1−e−λt
Between a and bP(a<T<b)=e−λa−e−λb
Percentilet=−ln(1−q)/λ
MemorylessnessP(T>s+t | T>s)=P(T>t)

Use matching units for rate and time. Round probabilities to four decimals and times to two decimals when necessary.
