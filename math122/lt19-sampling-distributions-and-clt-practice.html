Learning Target 19 · LT19

Sampling Distributions and CLT

Distinguish population and sampling distributions, compute the mean and standard error of a sample mean, and apply the Central Limit Theorem.

Quick reference

Sampling distributionDistribution of sample means from repeated random samples of size \(n\).
Mean\(\mu_{\bar X}=\mu\)
Standard error\(\sigma_{\bar X}=\frac{\sigma}{\sqrt n}\)
z-score\(z=\frac{\bar x-\mu}{\sigma/\sqrt n}\)

If the population is normal, sample means are exactly normal. Otherwise the CLT supports an approximately normal sampling distribution for sufficiently large independent samples with finite mean and variance. Strong skewness and outliers may require larger samples. For sampling without replacement, assume the population is at least 10 times the sample size. Round probabilities to four decimal places.
