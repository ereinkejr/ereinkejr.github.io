Types of Data

I can classify variables as categorical or quantitative and distinguish discrete from continuous quantitative variables.

Quick review

CategoricalGroups, labels, or identifiers. Numbers used only as codes still count as categorical.
Quantitative discreteCounts with separate possible values, usually whole numbers.
Quantitative continuousMeasurements that can take fractional values, even when rounded for recording.
