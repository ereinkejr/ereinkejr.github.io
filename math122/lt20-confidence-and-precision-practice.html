Confidence and Precision

Interpret confidence intervals and confidence levels and explain how sample size, variability, and confidence affect margin of error.

Quick review

Confidence interval\(\text{estimate}\pm E\)
Point estimate\(\frac{L+U}{2}\)
Margin of error\(E=\frac{U-L}{2}\)
Known-σ mean margin\(E=z^*\frac{\sigma}{\sqrt n}\)

A 95% confidence method captures the fixed population parameter in approximately 95% of intervals across repeated random samples, assuming its conditions are satisfied. Narrower intervals are more precise; larger samples generally reduce margin of error.
