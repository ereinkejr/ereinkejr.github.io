Learning Target 21 · LT21

Confidence Interval for a Mean

Select a one-sample z- or t-procedure, check conditions, construct and interpret confidence intervals for a population mean, and plan sample sizes.

Quick Review

Known population standard deviation\(\bar{x}\pm z^*\frac{\sigma}{\sqrt n}\)
Unknown population standard deviation\(\bar{x}\pm t^*\frac{s}{\sqrt n}\), with \(df=n-1\)
Margin of error\(E=(\text{critical value})(\text{standard error})\)
Planning sample size (known σ)\(n=\left(\frac{z^*\sigma}{E}\right)^2\); always round up

Two-sided z critical values: 90%: \(1.645\), 95%: \(1.960\), 99%: \(2.576\).

Conditions: random and independent observations; when sampling without replacement, \(n\) should be no more than 10% of the population. Small samples need an approximately normal population without serious outliers. Larger samples tolerate moderate departures, but severe skewness and extreme outliers still require care.
