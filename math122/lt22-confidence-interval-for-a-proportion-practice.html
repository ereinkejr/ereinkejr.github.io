Learning Target 22 · LT22

Confidence Interval for a Proportion

Check conditions, construct and interpret one-proportion confidence intervals, and plan sample sizes.

Quick review

Sample proportion\(\hat p=\frac{x}{n}\)
Estimated standard error\(SE=\sqrt{\frac{\hat p(1-\hat p)}{n}}\)
Margin of error\(E=z^*SE\)
Confidence interval\(\hat p\pm E\)
Sample-size planning\(n=\frac{(z^*)^2p^*(1-p^*)}{E^2}\)

Always round up.

ConditionsRandom and independent; without replacement, \(n\leq0.10N\); at least 10 observed successes and 10 failures.

Critical values

Use proportions as decimals. Round interval endpoints to three decimal places. A margin of error of 0.04 is 4 percentage points.
