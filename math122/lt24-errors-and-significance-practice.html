Learning Target 24 · LT24

Errors and Significance

Describe Type I and Type II errors in context, interpret significance levels, distinguish statistical significance from practical importance, and explain why failing to reject a null does not prove it true.

Quick Review

Type I errorReject a true \(H_0\).
Type II errorFail to reject a false \(H_0\).
Significance levelChoose \(\alpha\) before results; it controls Type I error risk when \(H_0\) is true and assumptions hold.
Statistical vs. practicalSignificance concerns evidence; practical importance concerns the effect's real-world value.

Reject \(H_0\) when \(p\text{-value}\leq\alpha\). Otherwise, fail to reject \(H_0\)—do not say it has been proven true.
