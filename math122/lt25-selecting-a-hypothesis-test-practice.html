Learning Target 25 · LT25

Selecting a Hypothesis Test

Choose and justify a test from the response variable, number of groups, sampling design, and test conditions. No test statistic or p-value calculation is required.

Quick Review

1. Response variableNumerical measurement → mean. Yes/no outcome → proportion.
2. Number of groupsOne population, two populations, or three or more independent groups?
3. Sampling designIndependent groups or paired measurements on the same individual?
4. ConditionsCheck sampling, independence, normality/outliers, and expected counts as appropriate.

Reference: usual hypothesis tests

Research situationUsual test
One population mean; σ knownOne-sample z-test for a mean
One population mean; σ unknownOne-sample t-test
One population proportionOne-proportion z-test
Two independent means; population SDs unknownIndependent two-sample t-test (Welch)
Paired numerical measurementsPaired t-test
Two independent proportionsTwo-proportion z-test
Three or more independent group meansOne-way ANOVA

A large sample does not correct biased selection. For sampling without replacement, assume population size is at least 10 times sample size unless stated otherwise. Paired yes/no data need a paired categorical procedure, not a numerical paired t-test.
