Learning Target 26 · LT26

Interpreting Hypothesis Test Results

Use supplied hypothesis test results to make a decision, justify it with a comparison, and write an appropriate conclusion about the population.

Quick Review

Reject the null\(p\text{-value}\leq\alpha\): reject \(H_0\). Sufficient evidence for \(H_a\).
Fail to reject\(p\text{-value}>\alpha\): fail to reject \(H_0\). Insufficient evidence for \(H_a\).
Not proofFailing to reject does not prove the null true. A p-value is not the probability that the null is true.
Scope of conclusionStatistical significance alone does not establish practical importance or causation.

Assume all conditions are satisfied. Use the supplied p-values; do not calculate test statistics or p-values.
