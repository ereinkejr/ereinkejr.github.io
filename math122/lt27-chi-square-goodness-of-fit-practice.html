Learning Target 27 · LT27

Chi-Square Goodness of Fit

Compare observed categorical counts with a specified distribution by finding expected counts, checking conditions, and interpreting a goodness-of-fit test.

Quick Review

Expected counts\(E_i=np_i\)
Test statistic\(\chi^2=\sum_i\frac{(O_i-E_i)^2}{E_i}\)
Degrees of freedom\(df=k-1\) for the specified distributions here.
Hypotheses\(H_0\): specified category probabilities hold.

\(H_a\): at least one differs.

ConditionsOne categorical variable; independent random observations; every expected count at least 5; sample at most 10% of population when sampling without replacement.
DecisionReject \(H_0\) when \(p\leq\alpha\); otherwise fail to reject. Default \(\alpha=0.05\).

A nonsignificant result does not prove the proposed distribution is true. A significant result does not identify which or how many categories differ.
