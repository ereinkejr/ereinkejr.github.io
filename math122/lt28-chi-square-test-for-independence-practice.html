Learning Target 28 · LT28

Chi-Square Test for Independence

Use a contingency table to find expected counts, check conditions, and interpret association between two categorical variables.

Quick Review

Hypotheses\(H_0:\) Variables independent in the population.

\(H_a:\) Variables associated in the population.

Expected counts\[E=\frac{(\text{row total})(\text{column total})}{n}\]
Test statistic\[\chi^2=\sum\frac{(O-E)^2}{E}\]
Degrees of freedom\[df=(r-1)(c-1)\]

Use random, independent observations; count each person once. Every expected count must be at least 5. Without replacement, sample at most 10% of population. Unless stated otherwise, use \(\alpha=0.05\), reject when \(p\leq\alpha\), and round test statistics to three decimals.
