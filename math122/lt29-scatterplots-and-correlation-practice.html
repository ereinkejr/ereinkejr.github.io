Scatterplots and Correlation

Describe direction, form, and strength; interpret the correlation coefficient; and explain why association alone does not establish causation.

Quick Review

ScatterplotEach point represents one observation with two quantitative measurements.
Describe the patternDirection, form (linear or curved), strength, and unusual observations.
Correlation\(-1\le r\le1\); measures direction and strength of linear association; no units.
LimitationsNear-zero correlation may hide curvature; unusual points can change \(r\); correlation does not establish causation.
