Sampling and Bias

Identify common sampling methods, recognize sources of bias, and explain when conclusions can be generalized to a population.

Quick reference

Simple randomEvery set of the chosen size has an equal chance.
SystematicRandom start, then every kth person.
StratifiedRandomly sample within every subgroup.
ClusterRandomly choose whole groups.
ConvenienceChoose people who are easy to reach.
Voluntary responsePeople opt in themselves.
UndercoverageSome population members are left out.
NonresponseSelected people do not respond.
Response biasWording or pressure distorts answers.
