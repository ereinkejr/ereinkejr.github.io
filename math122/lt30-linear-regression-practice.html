Linear Regression

Interpret fitted lines, predict responses, calculate residuals, interpret R², and recognize model limitations.

Quick Review

Fitted line\(\hat y=b_0+b_1x\)
Residual\(e=y-\hat y\)
Explained variation\(R^2=r^2\) for simple linear regression with an intercept
Interpolation vs. extrapolationWithin the observed x range vs. outside it.

Interpretation: The slope is change in predicted response per one x-unit. A positive residual is above the line. Correlation does not prove causation.
