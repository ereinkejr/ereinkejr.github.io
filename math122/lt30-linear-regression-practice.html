Learning Target 30 · LT30

Linear Regression

Interpret fitted lines, make predictions, calculate residuals, understand R², and recognize extrapolation and nonlinear patterns.

Quick Review

Fitted line\(\widehat y=b_0+b_1x\)
Residual\(e=y-\widehat y\)

Positive: observed above line. Negative: observed below.

Explained variation\(R^2=r^2\) for simple linear regression with an intercept.
Prediction rangeInterpolation is inside observed x-range; extrapolation is outside and needs caution.

Units: Slope is predicted change in response units for one explanatory-variable unit. The intercept predicts response at x=0, which may be outside the observed data. Correlation alone does not establish causation.
