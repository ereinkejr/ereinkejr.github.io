Learning Target 6 · LT6

Measures of Center

I can calculate and interpret the mean, median, and mode, explain how unusual values affect them, and choose an appropriate measure of center for a data set.

Quick review

MeanAdd the numerical values and divide by the number of values. It is sensitive to unusual observations.
MedianOrder the data and find the middle. For an even count, average the two middle values.
ModeThe most frequent value or category. A data set may have no mode or multiple modes.

A median or mean does not need to equal an observed value. For strongly skewed data or extreme outliers, the median often better represents a typical value.

Measures of center: formulas

Mean\[\bar{x}=\frac{\sum_{i=1}^{n}x_i}{n}\]
MedianThe middle ordered observation (or the average of the two middle observations).
ModeThe most frequently occurring value or category.
