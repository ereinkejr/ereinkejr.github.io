Learning Target 7 · LT7

Measures of Spread

Calculate and interpret range, variance, and standard deviation and use measures of spread to compare variability in context.

Quick reference

RangeMaximum − minimum
Sample variance\(s^2=\frac{\sum (x_i-\bar{x})^2}{n-1}\)
Sample SD\(s=\sqrt{s^2}\)
Population variance\(\sigma^2=\frac{\sum (x_i-\mu)^2}{N}\)
Population SD\(\sigma=\sqrt{\sigma^2}\)
UnitsVariance has squared units; SD uses the original units.

A calculator is welcome. Round variances and standard deviations to two decimal places when needed.
