Learning Target 8 · LT8

Relative Position and Boxplots

Interpret percentiles and quartiles, calculate IQR, flag potential outliers using the 1.5 IQR rule, and construct and compare modified boxplots.

Quick reference

QuartilesSort data first. Find Q1 and Q3 as medians of lower and upper halves; for odd n, exclude the overall median.
IQRIQR = Q3 − Q1; describes the middle approximately 50%.
Outlier fencesQ1 − 1.5(IQR) and Q3 + 1.5(IQR). Values strictly outside are flagged.
Modified boxplotWhiskers end at observed nonoutliers, not necessarily the fences. Plot outliers separately.
