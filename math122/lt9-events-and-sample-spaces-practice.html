Learning Target 9 · LT9

Events and Sample Spaces

I can describe a sample space, identify events and their complements, and determine probabilities from equally likely outcomes or observed relative frequencies.

Quick reference

Sample space SAll possible outcomes of an experiment.
Event AA collection of outcomes from S.
Complement \(A^c\)All outcomes in \(S\) that are not in \(A\). \(P(A^c)=1-P(A)\).
Equally likely outcomes\(P(A)=\frac{\#\text{ outcomes in }A}{\#\text{ outcomes in }S}\).
“And” / “Or”Intersection: both events. Union: at least one event.
Relative frequencyObserved event count divided by the total number of observations.

Important: Outcomes must be equally likely to use simple counting. Different colors or sums may not be equally likely even when individual tokens or ordered pairs are.
