Quick Reference

Basic objects
Point, line, plane, segment, and ray. A ray has one endpoint and extends indefinitely in one direction.
Angle measures
One complete turn is \(360^\circ\). Acute: less than \(90^\circ\); right: \(90^\circ\); obtuse: between \(90^\circ\) and \(180^\circ\).
Intersecting lines
Vertical angles are equal. Adjacent angles forming a straight line add to \(180^\circ\).
Parallel lines
Corresponding and alternate interior angles are equal; same-side interior angles are supplementary.
Polygons
Triangle: \(180^\circ\). Quadrilateral: \(360^\circ\). Pentagon: \(540^\circ\).

Geometry diagrams

Diagrams are schematic. Determine angles from stated relationships, not by measuring the drawings.

At the intersection, \(\angle 1=\angle 3\), \(\angle 2=\angle 4\), and adjacent angles total \(180^\circ\).

For parallel lines \(\ell\) and \(m\), corresponding angles (same relative position at each crossing) have equal measures.
