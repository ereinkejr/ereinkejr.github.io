Quick Reference

Circle

All points in a plane at a fixed distance from the center.

Sphere

All points in space at a fixed distance from the center.

Radius and diameter

\(d=2r\) and \(r=\frac d2\).

Chord

A segment whose endpoints lie on the circle. A diameter is a special chord.

Tools

A compass draws circles; a protractor measures angles.

Visualizing the lighthouse homework

Two lighthouses are one mile apart. Each circle has radius one mile. The shaded lens shows the region less than one mile from both lighthouses.

The circle boundaries themselves are excluded because the problem says “less than” one mile.
