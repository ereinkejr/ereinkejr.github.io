Quick Reference

Polygon
A simple, closed curve formed by three or more line segments.
Regular polygon
A polygon with all sides congruent and all angles congruent.
Diagonal
A segment joining two nonadjacent vertices of a polygon.
Interior-angle sum
For an \(n\)-sided polygon: \((n-2)\cdot180^\circ\).
Number of diagonals
For an \(n\)-sided polygon: \(\dfrac{n(n-3)}{2}\).
Triangle classifications
By sides: isosceles, equilateral, scalene. By angles: right, acute, obtuse.

A polygon must be both closed and simple (its sides do not cross). The formula for the interior-angle sum comes from dividing the polygon into \(n-2\) triangles.

Polygon Names

3 sides — Triangle
4 sides — Quadrilateral
5 sides — Pentagon
6 sides — Hexagon
7 sides — Heptagon

9 sides — Nonagon
