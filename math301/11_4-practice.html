Quick Reference: Conversion Factors

Use these relationships to build conversion factors. Choose the factor so the original unit cancels.

Length
\(1\) foot = \(12\) inches
\(1\) yard = \(3\) feet
\(1\) mile = \(5280\) feet
Capacity
\(1\) tablespoon = \(3\) teaspoons
\(1\) fluid ounce = \(2\) tablespoons
\(1\) cup = \(8\) fluid ounces
\(1\) pint = \(2\) cups
\(1\) quart = \(32\) fluid ounces
\(1\) gallon = \(4\) quarts
Mass
\(1\) pound = \(16\) ounces
\(1\) ton = \(2000\) pounds
\(1\) kilogram = \(2.2\) pounds
Metric and U.S. customary
\(1\) inch = \(2.54\) centimeters
\(1\) gallon = \(3.79\) liters
Metric prefixes
milli = \(\frac{1}{1000}\)
centi = \(\frac{1}{100}\)
kilo = \(1000\)
Temperature
\(C=\frac{5}{9}(F-32)\)
\(F=\frac{9}{5}C+32\)

Dimensional analysis: Multiply by a conversion factor equal to 1, arranging it so the unit you no longer need appears in both the numerator and denominator and cancels.

Key Ideas

Converting area

When converting square units, square the linear conversion factor. Since \(1\) yard = \(3\) feet, \(1\text{ yd}^2=3^2\text{ ft}^2=9\text{ ft}^2\).

Converting temperature

For Fahrenheit to Celsius, subtract \(32\) before multiplying by \(\frac{5}{9}\). For Celsius to Fahrenheit, multiply by \(\frac{9}{5}\) before adding \(32\).

Dimensions and measurement

Length is one-dimensional, area is two-dimensional, and volume is three-dimensional. Their units reflect the number of dimensions: units, square units, and cubic units, respectively.
