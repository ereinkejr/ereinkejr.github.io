Key Ideas

Moving Principle: Moving a region rigidly does not change its area.
Additivity Principle: If a region is dissected into non-overlapping pieces, the area of the whole region equals the sum of the areas of its pieces.

Area of a triangle

\[A=\frac{bh}{2}\]

Base: the length of a chosen side. Height: the perpendicular distance from that base to the opposite vertex. The height is not necessarily a slanted side.

Height is perpendicular to the base.
The perpendicular height can fall inside the triangle.
