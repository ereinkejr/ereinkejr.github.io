Quick Reference

Parallelogram
Area is base times perpendicular height: \(A=bh\).
Shearing
Shearing a parallelogram or triangle without changing its base or perpendicular height does not change its area.
Pick's Formula
For a simple polygon whose vertices lie on a unit grid, \(A=I+\frac{B}{2}-1\).
Perimeter and area
Perimeter measures boundary length; area measures the region covered. They are independent concepts.

The parallelograms above have the same base and perpendicular height, so they have the same area even though their shapes look different.

Grid-Based Polygon Practice

Pick's Formula applies when a simple polygon is drawn on a grid of lattice points one unit apart. Count the interior points \(I\) and boundary points \(B\), then substitute into the formula.

Remember: boundary points are counted once, including vertices. Interior points do not lie on an edge.
