Quick Reference

Polyhedron
A three-dimensional solid whose outer surface consists of polygons.
Face
A polygonal surface of a polyhedron.
Edge
A line segment where two faces meet.
Vertex
An endpoint where edges meet. The plural is vertices.
Prism
Has two congruent, parallel polygonal bases. Lateral faces are quadrilaterals.
Pyramid
Has one polygonal base; triangular faces meet at a common apex.
Right / oblique
A right prism has rectangular lateral faces. An oblique prism is tilted and has parallelogram lateral faces.
Euler's Formula
For a convex polyhedron, \(F-E+V=2\).

Important distinction: A sphere, cylinder, and cone are not polyhedra because their surfaces are not made entirely of polygonal faces.

General Counting Patterns

For a prism with an \(n\)-sided base:

\[ F=n+2,\qquad E=3n,\qquad V=2n. \]

For a pyramid with an \(n\)-sided base:

\[ F=n+1,\qquad E=2n,\qquad V=n+1. \]

Both patterns satisfy Euler's formula:

\[ F-E+V=2. \]
