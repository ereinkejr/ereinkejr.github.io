Quick Reference

Surface area
The sum of the areas of all outside faces or surfaces of a solid.
Net
A flat pattern that can be folded to construct a three-dimensional solid.
Faces
The polygons in a net that form the surfaces of a polyhedron.
Sphere
For radius \(r\), surface area is \(A=4\pi r^2\).
Cone
For radius \(r\) and slant height \(l\), total surface area is \(A=\pi rl+\pi r^2\).
Slant height
The distance along the sloping side of a cone from its vertex to the edge of its base.

Strategy: Identify every component of the surface, find each component's area, and add the areas. Keep track of whether a problem asks for total surface area or only lateral surface area.

Understanding Nets

A net represents all the surfaces of a solid laid flat. When folded along the edges, the pieces form the solid.

Schematic net of a right square pyramid. The four triangular faces surround the square base.

For a cylinder, a net consists of two congruent circles and one rectangle. For a cone, a net consists of one circle and one circular sector.
