Quick Reference

A transformation assigns every point in the plane to another point in the plane. Some points may remain fixed.

Reflection
Flips a figure across a line. A point and its image are the same perpendicular distance from the line of reflection.
Translation (slide)
Moves every point the same distance and in the same direction.
Rotation
Turns a figure around a fixed center through a specified angle.
Glide reflection
Combines a reflection with a translation parallel to the line of reflection.

An isometry preserves distances and angle measures. Reflections, translations, rotations, and glide reflections are isometries.

Coordinate Rules

Reflect across the x-axis
\((x,y)\rightarrow(x,-y)\)
Reflect across \(y=k\)
\((x,y)\rightarrow(x,2k-y)\)
Translate by \((a,b)\)
\((x,y)\rightarrow(x+a,y+b)\)
Rotate about the origin
90° counterclockwise: \((x,y)\rightarrow(-y,x)\)
90° clockwise: \((x,y)\rightarrow(y,-x)\)
180°: \((x,y)\rightarrow(-x,-y)\)

Isometries

An isometry is a transformation that preserves distances and angles. According to the Section 14.1 notes, every plane isometry is equivalent to one of four basic transformations: reflection, translation, rotation, or glide reflection.
