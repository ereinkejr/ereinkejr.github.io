Quick Reference

Statistics uses data to answer questions and learn about a group.

Statistics
The science of data. Its purpose is to learn something or answer a question.
Population
The entire group we want to learn about.
Sample
A subset of the population from which data are collected.
Quantitative data
Numerical data resulting from measuring or counting.
Categorical data
Qualitative data describing a characteristic or property.
Simple random sample
Each member of the population has an equal chance of selection.

Statistical process

  1. Formulate a question or state a hypothesis.
  2. Collect data.
  3. Analyze data.
  4. Interpret results and draw a conclusion.

Methods of collecting data

Observational studies, experiments (including control and treatment groups and placebos), and surveys.

Sampling methods

Random Stratified random Voluntary response Convenience

A random sample is more likely to represent the population. Biased questions or sampling methods can produce misleading data and false conclusions.

Thinking About Bias

A statistical question or sampling method is biased when it systematically favors certain responses or members of a population. A sample that is easy to obtain is not necessarily representative. Consider who had a chance to be selected and whether the wording of a question encourages a particular answer.
