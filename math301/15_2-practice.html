Quick Reference

Bar graph
Displays categorical data. Each bar represents a category; bars may be horizontal or vertical.
Pie chart
Displays categorical data as portions of a whole. All sectors represent 100% together.
Histogram
Displays numerical data grouped into intervals (bins). Adjacent bars touch.
Boxplot
Displays the five-number summary: minimum, first quartile, median, third quartile, and maximum.
Line graph
Displays change over time. A time series is plotted in chronological order.
Scatterplot
Displays paired numerical data for two variables and can reveal association.
Normal distribution
A symmetric, bell-shaped distribution centered at its mean.

Normal Distributions

A normal distribution is symmetric and bell-shaped. In an ideal normal distribution, the mean, median, and mode coincide at the center. The standard deviation describes spread: a larger standard deviation produces a wider, flatter curve.
