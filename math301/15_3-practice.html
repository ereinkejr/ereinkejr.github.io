Quick Reference

Mean

\(\displaystyle \overline{x}=\frac{x_1+x_2+\cdots+x_N}{N}\)

Add all values and divide by the number of values.
Median

The middle value after arranging the data in order. For an even number of values, average the two middle values.

Mode

The value that occurs most often.

Range

\(\text{maximum}-\text{minimum}\)

Interquartile range

\(\mathrm{IQR}=Q_3-Q_1\)

Standard deviation

Measures typical distance from the mean; in these notes, \(s=\sqrt{\text{mean of squared deviations}}\).

Percentiles and quartiles

A score in the \(p^{\text{th}}\) percentile is greater than or equal to \(p\) percent of the scores. The median is the 50th percentile, \(Q_1\) is the 25th percentile, and \(Q_3\) is the 75th percentile.

Empirical rule for a normal distribution

For an approximately bell-shaped normal distribution, about 68% of values lie within 1 standard deviation of the mean, 95% within 2 standard deviations, and 99.7% within 3 standard deviations.
