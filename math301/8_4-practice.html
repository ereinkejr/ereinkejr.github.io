Key ideas from the notes

A prime number is a counting number greater than 1 whose only positive factors are 1 and itself. The number 1 is not prime.

Determining primality: To decide whether \(n\) is prime, test divisibility by prime numbers \(p\leq\sqrt n\).

Fundamental Theorem of Arithmetic: Every counting number greater than 1 has a unique prime factorization, apart from the order of factors.

Infinitude of primes: There are infinitely many prime numbers.
