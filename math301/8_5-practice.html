Quick reference

The greatest common factor (GCF, also called GCD) is the largest counting number dividing each number. The least common multiple (LCM) is the smallest positive multiple shared by the numbers.

For prime factorizations, the GCF uses the smaller exponent of each shared prime, while the LCM uses the larger exponent of every prime present.

\[\gcd(a,b)\cdot\operatorname{lcm}(a,b)=ab\]

In the slide method, repeatedly divide both numbers by a common factor. Multiply the factors used on the left to find the GCF; multiply those factors by the two remaining entries to find the LCM.
