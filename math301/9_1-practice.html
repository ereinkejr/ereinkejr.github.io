Quick Reference

A numerical expression is a string of numbers and operation symbols. Evaluate using parentheses, exponents, multiplication/division from left to right, then addition/subtraction from left to right.

For example, \(2+3-4\cdot5=5-20=-15\).

Visual counting strategies often use equal groups, additive decomposition, or subtraction of overlaps. For shaded areas, products of fractions can describe portions of strips within a rectangle.
