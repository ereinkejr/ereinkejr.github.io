Quick Reference

A variable represents a number; a coefficient multiplies a variable. For example, in \(5x+3\), the coefficient of \(x\) is \(5\).
To evaluate an expression, substitute a value for its variables. Two expressions are equivalent when they have the same value for every substitution.
Order matters: Removing \(\frac14\) of an amount and then adding \(\frac23\) gives \(\frac34x+\frac23\); adding first and then removing a quarter gives \(\frac34(x+\frac23)\).
