Quick Reference

A sequence is an ordered list of terms. The term in position \(n\) is \(a_n\). Counting starts at \(n=1\).

Arithmetic sequence

Add the same difference \(d\) each time.

\[a_n=a_1+(n-1)d\]
Geometric sequence

Multiply by the same ratio \(r\) each time.

\[a_n=a_1r^{n-1}\]
Fibonacci sequence

Each new term is the sum of the two preceding terms.

\[1,1,2,3,5,8,13,\ldots\]
Repeating patterns

Divide the position by the cycle length and use the remainder to locate the shape.

Study Tip

For an arithmetic sequence, check the difference between consecutive terms. For a geometric sequence, check the ratio. For a repeating shape pattern, identify the length of one complete cycle before finding a distant term.
